Research Areas

Generative modeling for medical imaging — and whether the results can be trusted

Cardiac MRI

Recovering full 3D cardiac anatomy from sparse acquisitions: work with NVIDIA interpolates in a diffusion model's latent space, while a parallel line reads cardiac function straight from undersampled k-space.

  • k-Space
  • Diffusion Models
  • 3D Reconstruction

Generative AI

Where does reconstruction end and generation begin? My DGM4MICCAI work maps that spectrum for cardiac MRI; related projects grow glioblastoma under physics-guided constraints, so tumour dynamics drive the image.

  • Image Synthesis
  • Physics-Guided
  • Autoregressive Generation

Tokenization

Compressing medical volumes into compact discrete representations generative models can use directly. How far can compression go before anatomical fidelity breaks down? Publication forthcoming.

  • Autoencoders
  • Discrete Representations
  • High Compression

Trustworthy Medical AI

Medical AI that clears a benchmark can still fail patients. My red-teaming agents probe medical language models for what benchmarks miss (Nature Health, 2026); with Harvard Medical School I study how reconstruction shapes fairness.

  • Red-Teaming
  • Fairness
  • Evaluation
